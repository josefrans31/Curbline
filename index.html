Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Curbline ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Curbline is a parking and driving game. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your career progress, coin balance, owned cars, car customizations, driver name and sound setting are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data, or tapping "Reset progress", deletes them.

What other players see in online mode

When you join online free drive, the game shares the following with the other players connected at the same moment:

  • the driver name you type in (use a nickname, not your real name)
  • which car you drive and how it is customized
  • your car's position, direction and speed in the game city

This is sent live and is not stored. It disappears when you leave online mode or close the game. Solo modes and career never share anything.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ad tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and have no cash value.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts or real-world location.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Curbline is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online mode only shares a nickname and in-game car data, and players under 13 should use it with a parent's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
