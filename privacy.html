Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Curbline ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Curbline is a parking and driving game. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your career progress, coin balance, owned cars, car customizations, daily challenge results, driver name and settings (sound, steering, time of day, police level and similar) are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data, or tapping "Reset progress" in the game menu, deletes your progress.

Online mode

Online free drive is switched off for now, so the game does not share anything with other players. If it returns, it will share only these things, live and without storing them, with the players connected at the same moment:

  • the driver name you type in (use a nickname, not your real name)
  • which car you drive and how it is customized
  • your car's position, direction and speed in the game city

Game controllers

If you connect a game controller, the game reads its buttons and sticks so you can drive with it. Nothing about the controller is stored or sent anywhere.

How the game reaches you

Curbline is a web page. As with any website, the services that deliver its files receive standard technical details such as your IP address and browser type when your browser downloads them. Those services are our web host (Netlify), Google Fonts for the typefaces, and the cdnjs library network (run by Cloudflare) for the 3D graphics library. We do not use those details to identify you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ad tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and have no cash value.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts or real-world location.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Curbline is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Send us a message with this form. If you want a reply, leave an email address. The message and the address are used only to answer you, and we delete them afterwards.
